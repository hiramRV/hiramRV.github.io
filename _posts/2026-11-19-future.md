# Everything I need 

I have been faced with one difficult question lately, **what will happen in the future?** And even though it is a practical question, because it refers to the immediate future and people are inquiring about vacation plans, holidays, and work, I cannot answer any of that now. And don't get me wrong, I would love to be able to answer back, but I have spent more time adjusting plans than actually following. 

How do I feel about this? Tired and bored, again. Since more effort does not seem to work, and because I like to feel that I'm trying anyway, what else can I do? Give up on plans and live a moment-to-moment life? Quit everything and rest until I have the energy that I need? Read and try to find my way out of it? Yes! 

Since an escape seems to be the only way, I started reading a book called **Why I am a Stoic** by **Seneca**, and one idea seems to have hit a spot: *The Wise Man is Content with Himself*. The idea is not as simple as it seems, because we are talking about a deep idea that has been around for a long time. What do we need? Do we need people? Are we incomplete alone? But the more I think about it, the more I come back to the question about the future. How would I like to feel when asked this question? What can I learn about this small chapter? 

One key idea that I want to nurture now is this feeling of completeness, of feeling whole and at peace. I want to look at the sky and feel that I lack nothing, that everything that I need now, and that I will need in the future, is already part of me. I don't believe this implies getting rid of all my possessions, friends, or hobbies, or that it is simple inner speech that I need to give myself every time I have a difficulty in life; I think this is an understanding of life. 

So people can take away my possessions, house, visa, friends, family, name, and body. But what I have with me has never been reachable by anyone else to take. 

<p style="text-align: center">Tengo en mi todo lo que necesito.</p>

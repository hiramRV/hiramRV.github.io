# One day, you just wake up

Like today, I just woke up and felt only one thing: Tired. 

Tired of having to solve so many things every single day just to exist in this world. Which does not make sense, right? 

Just let me snap out of it and enjoy things as I used to. I want enjoy the sun, get sleepy, and nap under the ski. That, just, enjoy. 

Days like today I forget how simple I wish for life to be. 

Then I question if the life that I live and the meaning that I give it, is actually correct. 

Reflections like this are also, just, exhausting. 

So, just for today, I will rest.
